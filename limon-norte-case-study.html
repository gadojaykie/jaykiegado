Project goal

The project brings resident registration and feedback online, while giving staff a place to handle requests, keep records, and respond.

What was delivered

  • Online resident registration
  • A channel for resident feedback and requests
  • Staff tools for managing submitted requests and records
  • A way for staff to respond to residents

Implementation

The portal uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for its interface, with PHP and MySQL for its server-side and data features.
